💛 Our Mission

Every shoe deserves a sole mate.

When someone loses a leg, they're often left with a brand new, unworn shoe — the one for the foot they no longer have. It sits in a box. It's a small thing, but it stings.

SoleBuddies is a free community where amputees can list that unused shoe and find someone who lost the opposite leg — in the same size. A simple trade. Two people walk away with what they need.

No money changes hands. No middleman. Just a community taking care of each other. 💛

✨ How SoleBuddies Works ✨

1

List Your Shoe

Add your shoe's brand, size, gender sizing, which foot, and your location. Takes 60 seconds.

2

Find a Match

We highlight listings where someone has the opposite shoe in your exact size. ✨ = great match.

3

Message & Trade

Use the built-in messaging to connect and sort out the swap — no email needed.

4

Mark as Traded

Once you've arranged your swap, mark the listing as traded so others know it's taken.
