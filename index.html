💛 Our Mission

Every shoe deserves a sole buddy.

When someone loses a leg, they're often left with a brand new, unworn shoe — the one for the foot they no longer have. It sits in a box. It's a small thing, but it stings.

SoleBuddies is a free community where amputees can list that unused shoe and find someone who lost the opposite leg — in the same size. A simple trade. Two people walk away with what they need.

No money changes hands. No middleman. Just a community taking care of each other. 💛
